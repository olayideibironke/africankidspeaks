\# Privacy Policy – AfricanKidSpeaks



Last updated: January 2026



AfricanKidSpeaks is an educational language-learning app designed for children and families.



\## Information We Collect

AfricanKidSpeaks does \*\*not\*\* collect, store, or share any personal data.

\- No names

\- No email addresses

\- No phone numbers

\- No precise location data

\- No photos or videos

\- No biometric data



\## Children’s Privacy

AfricanKidSpeaks is designed for children under 13 and complies with COPPA and Google Play Families policies.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.



\## Audio \& Speech

The app plays \*\*pre-generated audio files\*\* bundled within the app.

Text-to-speech may be used \*\*locally on the device\*\* as a fallback.

No audio data is transmitted or stored externally.



\## Permissions

AfricanKidSpeaks does not require:

\- Camera access

\- Microphone access

\- Contacts access

\- Location access



\## Third-Party Services

AfricanKidSpeaks does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or trackers.



\## Data Security

Since no personal data is collected, there is no personal data stored or shared.
